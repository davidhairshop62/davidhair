1

Cost Architecture

Men's toupees rely on short-lifespan bases and bi-weekly adhesive salon refits, producing high ongoing recurring costs. Women's toppers carry high initial investment but low monthly costs when maintained properly with clips.

2

Bleached Knots & Shedding

Bleaching knots eliminates dark speckles on lace and mono bases for supreme naturalness, but weakens the hair roots. Expect up to 30% reduction in base lifespan compared to unbleached alternatives.

3

Density Calibrations

The #1 sign of a hairpiece is excess density. Men should target 80% to 100% density to blend with bio sides. Women typically require 110% to 130% to achieve natural crown volume without overwhelming the head.
